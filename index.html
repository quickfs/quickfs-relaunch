A note from Ted Cooper

After much prayer, I have decided not to relaunch QuickFS.

However, I have introduced Benjamin at FastFS.org to a vendor who can supply high-quality data on terms that would allow him to recreate QuickFS with all major features. I've been impressed by the manner with which Benjamin has navigated challenges with FastFS -- choosing to act with integrity rather than taking shortcuts. I have confidence that he will deliver a worthy successor to QuickFS.

It means a lot to me that so many of you reached out to express your gratitude for QuickFS. I have learned a lot from this experience and hearing how much people loved the product was most gratifying for me. I hope to make the same impact on people in whatever I do going forward.
